# Contributing to Kegare

Thanks for wanting to help. Kegare's code is under the MIT License and its assets are All Rights Reserved (see the License
section of the README), so please read this before opening a pull request.

## Your rights in what you contribute

By submitting a contribution (code, art, sounds, text or anything else) to this repository, you agree that:

- you have the right to submit it: it's your own work, or you have permission, and it doesn't copy anyone else's mod or assets;
- you grant the Kegare owner a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free right to use, change, distribute and
  relicense it as part of Kegare or anything that comes from it.
- you won't be paid for it, and it may be changed or removed at any time.

If you can't agree to this, please don't open a pull request.

## Before you start

- **Open an issue first for anything bigger than a small fix.** Kegare follows its own design (DESIGN.md); large
  changes that weren't discussed will likely be closed, however good they are.
- **Keep it small and focused.** One change per pull request.
- **Tests must pass:** `./gradlew test`, and `./gradlew runData` must leave no changes.

## AI-assisted contributions

They're welcome, but you're responsible for them: you must have read and understood every line you submit.
